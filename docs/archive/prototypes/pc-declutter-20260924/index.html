AFocused library

레일은 에셋·컬렉션·망가 3개. 나머지는 이동(Ctrl K)과 더보기.

에셋 · concept-a.html#library 이동 팔레트 · #command 캐릭터 시리즈 · #characters 설정 › 고급 복구·진단 · #settings

Strengths

  • Fewest permanent controls; the gallery gets the most calm space.
  • One place to find every hidden feature; queues surface only when non-zero.

Costs

  • 메모·비밀·다시보기 lose one-click rail access.
  • A palette is a new interaction to learn and build; it must not pretend to be asset text search.

BTask-first home

새 홈 화면: 이어 보기, 확인할 큐, 다시보기. 도구는 한 줄로.

홈 · concept-b.html#home 에셋 · #library 설정 › 동기화 · #settings

Strengths

  • The most-used flows get the biggest targets; review queues become visible work, not a "!" badge.
  • Maps directly to mobile's "one screen = one conclusion".

Costs

  • Adds a destination and data (resume position, queue counts) that do not exist yet.
  • Risk of the dashboard/card wall DESIGN.md warns against; must stay media-led.

CQuiet chrome

Strengths

  • Lowest risk: same areas and muscle memory, mostly relocation.
